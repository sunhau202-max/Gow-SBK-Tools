WHAT IT CAN DO
  - Edit the audio inside SBK / SBP banks in God of War 1 and God of War 2 (PS2) WAD files: play,
    export to WAV, replace with your own WAV.
  - Open GoW 1 / GoW 2 ISOs (PART1.PAK / PART2.PAK -> WAD list through GODOFWAR.TOC), a single
    WAD, or a folder of WADs; save straight into the ISO (every copy) with a backup and Restore ISO.
  - Built-in SBK catalog: 385 SBKs (GoW 1: 200 SBKs in 155 WADs; GoW 2: 185 SBKs in 185 WADs).
    The full list is in Katalog_SBK.txt.
  - Two bank types: internal audio banks and VAG banks (VAG files already packed inside the SBK).
  - Three languages: Indonesia, English, Português (Brasil).

NUMBERS
  - GoW 1: 216 WADs scanned; 200 SBKs contain audio (155 WADs). 7 SBKs are references only / empty.
  - GoW 2: 293 WADs scanned; 185 SBKs contain audio (185 WADs). 16 SBKs are references only.
  - WADs that were scanned and have no SBK with audio are hidden in the ISO tree.
  Coverage is limited to the WADs the author scanned. Other WADs still show in the ISO but are
  not guaranteed.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO / OUT OF SCOPE
  - Standalone .VPK or .VAG files on the disc, music / dialogue streams outside the WADs, video /
    cutscenes (PSS, MPEG-2), 3D models, textures, and in-game language text.
  - SBKs that hold no audio of their own (reference lists only).
  - Rebuilding ISOs, or opening loose PART*.PAK files outside an ISO.
  - Enlarging a WAD that lives inside an ISO (a WAD inside an ISO must keep its size).

TECHNICAL NOTES
  - Replacing with a WAV of the same length or shorter does not change the WAD size and does not
    shift any other file.
  - The Enlarge mode (loose WADs only) changes the WAD size and shifts what follows. The tool first
    checks that the references in the SBK header can be shifted safely; if not, enlarging is
    refused. In random tests about 1 in 10 samples was refused.
  - WAD location in the ISO: GoW 1 uses a PART number + sector (one WAD is listed several times);
    GoW 2 uses a location table (it may hold more than one copy). Every copy is written.
  - ISO backup: NAME.iso.sbkbak. Language choice: sbk_tools_settings.json. Errors:
    gow_tool_error.txt (next to the exe).
  - The PS2 audio format (PS-ADPCM) is re-encoded from your WAV (mono; stereo mixed down; rate adapted).

TEST STATUS
  The data structures were tested automatically on every scanned WAD (replace and enlarge). The
  ISO/TOC reader was tested with simulated ISOs built from the real GoW 1 and GoW 2 GODOFWAR.TOC
  files and real WADs (WAD lists, reading, writing every copy, backup and restore matched). The
  author also tested results in-game on a number of GoW 1 and GoW 2 SBKs (loose WADs). Writing
  straight into a real game ISO has NOT yet been tested on a real device. Use with care and always
  on a copy.
