WHAT IT CAN DO
  - Edit the audio inside SBK / SBP banks in God of War 1 and God of War 2
    (PS2) WAD files: play, export to WAV, replace with your own WAV.
  - Detects GoW 1 / GoW 2 automatically and shows it in the status bar.
  - Built-in SBK catalog: 385 SBKs (GoW 1: 200 SBKs in 155 WADs; GoW 2: 185 SBKs
    in 185 WADs). The full list is in Katalog_SBK.txt.
  - Two bank types are supported: internal audio banks and VAG banks (VAG
    files already packed inside the SBK).
  - Three languages: Indonesia, English, Português (Brasil).

NUMBERS
  Scan results used to build this tool:
  - GoW 1: 216 WADs; 200 SBKs contain audio (155 WADs). 7 SBKs only hold
    references or are empty (no audio) and are hidden.
  - GoW 2: 292 WADs; 185 SBKs contain audio (185 WADs). 16 SBKs only hold
    references (no audio) and are hidden.
  - WADs without an SBK do not need to be opened in this tool.
  The coverage above is limited to the WADs the author scanned. WADs that were
  never scanned cannot be guaranteed.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO / OUT OF SCOPE
  - Opening PAK, TOC or ISO files (.WAD files only; huge files are refused).
  - Standalone .VPK or .VAG files on the disc.
  - Music / dialogue streams stored outside the WADs.
  - Video / cutscenes (PSS, MPEG-2).
  - 3D models (glTF), textures, and in-game language text.
  - SBKs that hold no audio of their own (reference lists only).

TECHNICAL NOTES
  - Replacing with a WAV of the same length or shorter does not change the
    WAD size and does not shift any other file.
  - The Enlarge mode changes the WAD size and shifts what follows. The tool
    first checks that the references in the SBK header can be shifted safely;
    if not, enlarging is refused and the WAD is left untouched. In random
    tests about 1 in 10 samples was refused.
  - After the WAD grows, the ISO / PAK must be rebuilt with another tool.
  - The PS2 audio format (PS-ADPCM) is re-encoded by the tool from your WAV
    (mono; stereo WAVs are mixed down; the sample rate is adapted).
  - The language choice is stored in sbk_tools_settings.json (next to the
    exe). Errors are logged to gow_tool_error.txt.

TEST STATUS
  The data structures were tested automatically on every scanned WAD
  (replace and enlarge). The author also tested results in-game on a number of
  GoW 1 and GoW 2 SBKs. Not every SBK was tested one by one in the game, and
  the Enlarge mode may crash the game if it grows too much. Use with care and
  always on a copy.
